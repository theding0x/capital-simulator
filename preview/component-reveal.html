Social relations of linen

The exchange of commodities is, behind the back of the producers, an exchange of human labour-times taking the form of a thing.

30 min of abstract human labour are congealed in one yard of linen, produced by weaving.

  • 1 yard linen ≡ 0.05 coats — both expressing 30 min of abstract human labour (weaving ↔ tailoring)
  • 1 yard linen ≡ 0.0125 quarter corn — both expressing 30 min of abstract human labour (weaving ↔ cultivating)
